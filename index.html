Our story

It started with my own kids

I'm a mom of two, and I wanted my kids to have a real chance to show what they can do. When it was time to help them prepare, I went looking for practice tests. What I found was expensive and full of errors, and there was no way to see the materials before paying. You paid first and found out later whether it was any good.

So I took their education into my own hands. I used my tech background to build my own materials, on nights after the kids were in bed and on weekends.

It worked. The questions were clear. I could print them and let my kids work at their own pace. Every answer came with a clear explanation.

Then friends started asking if I would share what I had made for my kids. I did, and I saw it work for their children too.

That is when I knew this was bigger than my own family, and I decided to share it with the world. Every child deserves the chance to succeed, and the best materials to prepare for the future.

Archana, mom and founder of Cognita Kids

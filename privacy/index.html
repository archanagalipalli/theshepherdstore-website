Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

Cognita Kids is operated by The Shepherd Store LLC ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Cognita Kids app and the website theshepherdstore.com.

Who our services are for

Our app is designed for parents and guardians to use with their children. Accounts and purchases are made by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

What we collect

  • From the app: the grade level you select, each child's nickname, and practice answers and scores. These are stored on your device. If iCloud backup is on (it is by default, and you can turn it off in the app's Settings), a copy is also kept in your own iCloud account with Apple, so it comes back after reinstalling or on a new iPhone or iPad with the same Apple Account. That copy is between you and Apple: we can't see it.
  • Purchase status: when you subscribe, Apple processes the payment. We receive confirmation that a subscription is active, linked to an anonymous identifier. We never see your card details.
  • Name and email address (optional): if you contact us, ask to hear when the app launches, or ask for progress reports.
  • Crash and error reports: if the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report with the error, the app and iOS version, the device model, and which screen was open, so we can fix it. Reports contain no names, no child nickname, no answers and no device identifiers. They are handled by Sentry. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • Things that stay on your phone: reminders are scheduled on your device, and the natural voice is recorded ahead of time and built into the app, so neither sends anything.
  • Problem reports and cancellation feedback: when you tap "Report a problem" on a question and pick a reason, the app sends us the question details (grade, test, question number, the answer key and the answer picked) and the app version. If you cancel a plan and choose to tell us why, the app sends your answer, the plan, the grade and the app version. Both are emailed to us through Cloudflare and Resend and contain no names, email addresses or device identifiers. Choosing "Something else" opens our support form with the question details filled in instead; nothing is sent unless you submit that form.
  • From the website: standard technical information that our website host (GitHub Pages) records when you visit, such as your IP address. The website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for a child's name, photo, birthday, school, or location. We do not show ads. We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

How we use information

To provide practice and score reports, manage subscriptions, answer support requests, send updates you asked for, and fix bugs.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run the app and site, and only for that purpose: Apple (payments and app distribution), RevenueCat (subscription management), Sentry (crash and error reports), Web3Forms (delivers messages sent through our contact form), Cloudflare and Resend (deliver the app's problem reports and cancellation feedback to us by email), GoDaddy (email), and GitHub (website hosting). We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Practice data stays on your device, and in your iCloud backup if backup is on, until you remove it. You can do that in the app at any time: "Remove" a child on the Who's practicing? screen erases that child's scores, medals and progress, and Settings → "Erase everything" erases every child and all progress. With iCloud backup on, both also erase it from your iCloud backup and from your other iPhones and iPads that use that backup. With iCloud backup off, they erase this device only; you can remove an iCloud copy by turning backup on first, or in iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage. Deleting the app removes the data on that device but not the iCloud backup. We keep email addresses until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, and purchase records for as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information we hold by sending us a note through our contact form. You can unsubscribe from emails with the link in any message. Parents and guardians can ask us at any time to delete any information relating to their child. Children's nicknames, answers and scores are never sent to us, so you delete those yourself in the app (see "How long we keep it").

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the effective date.

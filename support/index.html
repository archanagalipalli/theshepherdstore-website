Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Cognita Kids, and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund? Apple handles all App Store refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase. If you bought a printable on Etsy or Amazon, request the refund through that store.

I bought a subscription but the app still shows the free version. Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Which grades and tests do you cover? Our printable practice tests cover the CogAT from kindergarten through grade 9. The app covers CogAT-style practice for kindergarten through grade 8. More tests and grades are planned.

How do I delete my data? Practice results are stored on your device and are removed when you delete the app. To remove an email address you gave us, send us a note with the contact form above.
